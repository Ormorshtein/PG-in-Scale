1. Cross-DC support and latency concerns
We don't believe this is supported across data centers. Lev also explained to them yesterday why — there are latency issues involved.
2. Handling network blips and flapping
We asked how the system handles momentary network disconnections or jitter. The clusters could start flapping between sites, and we'd run into even more issues — similar to what happened this week with our critical system. Failovers would occur and the application wouldn't handle them gracefully, potentially causing more severe damage than the original issue.
3. Data loss during failover
What about data loss? Do we failover at all costs, regardless of potential data loss?
4. Justification for an additional tool
What is the benefit of maintaining yet another tool? Why not write a migration/failover script and expose it through our SRE team or through an existing enablement platform? Oracle also has an automatic failover option, but it was never implemented in our environment — and I believe there's a good reason for that.
